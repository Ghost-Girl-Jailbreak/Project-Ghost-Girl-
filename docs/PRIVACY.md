# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

---

## Short Version

**Project Ghost Girl collects no personal data.**

No accounts. No telemetry. No tracking. No ads. No data selling.

---

## What the Client Sends

The client makes two kinds of network requests:

1. **Fetching the manifest** (`repo.ini`) from `raw.githubusercontent.com`
2. **Downloading app files** (`.xex`) from `raw.githubusercontent.com`

Each request includes:

- An HTTP GET request
- A user agent string (`GhostGirl/<version>`)

**Nothing else.** No console ID, no profile name, no IP address
sent to us (GitHub may log IPs as part of normal hosting — see
[GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement)).

---

## What the Client Stores

Locally on your console:

| Location | Contents |
|---|---|
| `Hdd:\Apps\` | Installed apps |
| `Hdd:\GhostGirl\cache\` | Temporary downloads |
| `Hdd:\GhostGirl\ghostgirl.log` | Log file |

All of this is **on your console**. Nothing leaves it unless you
send it somewhere yourself.

---

## What We Do NOT Collect

- ❌ No personal information
- ❌ No console serial numbers
- ❌ No profile names
- ❌ No IP addresses (beyond GitHub's own logging)
- ❌ No usage statistics
- ❌ No crash reports
- ❌ No analytics
- ❌ No device fingerprints

---

## Third-Party Services

The only external service the client contacts is **GitHub**, via
`raw.githubusercontent.com`. GitHub's privacy practices apply to
that traffic — see their privacy statement linked above.

We do not integrate with:

- Google Analytics
- Facebook
- Any ad network
- Any tracking service
