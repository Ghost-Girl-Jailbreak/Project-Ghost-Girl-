Project Ghost Girl is **proprietary**. All rights reserved.

- You may **view** the source code

- You may **not** fork it for your own distribution

### Not Affiliated

Project Ghost Girl is not affiliated with Microsoft, Sony, Nintendo, or any console manufacturer.

### Modding Risk

Modding your console:

- Voids warranties
- Carries risk of bricking
- May be illegal in some jurisdictions

**You proceed at your own risk.**

### Privacy

- No personal data collected
- No telemetry
- No analytics
- No tracking

### Piracy

Project Ghost Girl is for **homebrew apps**, not commercial games. We do not condone or support piracy in any form.
