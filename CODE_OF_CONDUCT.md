# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Project Ghost Girl to be a welcoming place for anyone
interested in console homebrew — regardless of experience level,
console preference, or background.

## Our Standards

**Expected behavior:**

- Be respectful and patient with newcomers
- Give constructive feedback, not personal attacks
- Accept that maintainers have final say on project decisions
- Respect the project's proprietary license
- Keep discussions on-topic in their respective channels

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, hate speech, or discrimination of any kind
- Sharing piracy links or copyrighted ROMs
- Leaking unreleased builds, private files, or dev conversations
- Spamming, self-promotion, or DM advertising
- Doxxing or sharing anyone's personal information
- Deliberately derailing threads or starting drama

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- The GitHub repository (issues, PRs, discussions)
- The Project Ghost Girl Discord server
- Any other official community space

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:

1. A warning from a moderator
2. Temporary mute or ban from community spaces
3. Permanent removal from all Project Ghost Girl spaces

Maintainers have final say on all moderation decisions.

## Reporting

To report a violation:

- **Discord:** DM a moderator directly
- **GitHub:** Open a private report or contact a maintainer

Reports are handled confidentially. False reports made in bad
faith may themselves be treated as violations.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted for Project Ghost Girl and is
not a copy of any standard CoC. It is intentionally short and
direct.
